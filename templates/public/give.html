Where Your Money Goes

We are committed to transparency and stewardship. Here's how your generous contributions are used.

Tithe (10%)

Supporting the local church

Your tithes support the day-to-day operations of the church including staff salaries, utilities, building maintenance, and ministry programs that serve our congregation.

Offering

Above and beyond the tithe

Offerings support special projects, building funds, community outreach programs, and emergency assistance for members in need.

Missions

Spreading the Gospel globally

Mission donations support our missionaries, church planting efforts, and humanitarian projects in communities around the world.

Community Outreach

Serving our local community

Outreach giving funds food drives, medical camps, educational sponsorships, and other initiatives that serve the less privileged in our community.
