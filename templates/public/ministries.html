Our Ministries

Discover your place of service and community. Every member has a gift and a purpose in the body of Christ.

Serving Together

Find Your Place to Serve

At Grace Church Munyaka, we believe every believer is uniquely gifted and called to serve. Our ministries provide opportunities for you to grow, connect, and make a difference in the lives of others.

Adult Ministries

Men's Ministry

Building godly men who lead their families, churches, and communities with integrity, courage, and faith. Through mentorship, retreats, and accountability groups, we equip men to fulfill their God-given calling.

Meeting: Last Saturday of every month, 8:00 AM

Women's Ministry

Empowering women of all ages to walk in their God-given identity, purpose, and destiny. We offer Bible studies, conferences, mentorship programs, and community outreach initiatives.

Meeting: Every Saturday, 9:00 AM

Couples & Family

Strengthening marriages and families through biblical teaching, counseling, retreats, and fellowship. We help couples build thriving relationships anchored in God's Word.

Meeting: 2nd Friday of every month, 7:00 PM

Senior Saints

Honoring and ministering to our senior members through fellowship, prayer support, and activities that celebrate their wisdom and contribution to the church family.

Meeting: Every Thursday, 10:00 AM

Bible Study Groups

Small group Bible studies meeting in homes and at the church throughout the week. Dive deep into Scripture in a supportive community of fellow believers.

Meeting: Various times and locations

Outreach & Missions

Taking the Gospel beyond our walls through community service, evangelism, and supporting missionaries locally and internationally.

Meeting: 1st Saturday of every month, 7:00 AM

Youth & Children's Ministries

Youth Ministry

For young people aged 13-25. We create a dynamic environment where youth encounter God, build lasting friendships, and develop their God-given potential through worship, teaching, and social events.

Meeting: Saturdays, 3:00 PM

Children's Church

Age-appropriate worship, Bible lessons, and activities for children ages 3-12. We create a safe, fun environment where children fall in love with Jesus and His Word.

Meeting: Every Sunday during both services

Teen Connect

A vibrant group for teenagers (13-17) navigating the challenges of adolescence with faith. Through relevant teaching, mentorship, and fun activities, we help teens build a strong foundation in Christ.

Meeting: Fridays, 6:00 PM

Worship & Creative Arts

Choir

Our church choir leads the congregation in powerful worship through anthems, hymns, and contemporary gospel music. All voices are welcome.

Rehearsal: Thursdays, 7:00 PM

Praise & Worship Team

A team of vocalists and instrumentalists who lead the congregation into God's presence through contemporary worship. Auditions held quarterly.

Rehearsal: Wednesdays, 6:00 PM & Saturdays, 2:00 PM

Media & Tech Team

Behind-the-scenes team handling sound, lighting, livestream, photography, and social media. Use your technical skills to advance the Kingdom.

Meeting: Tuesdays, 6:00 PM

We'd love to help you find the perfect ministry fit. Reach out to our pastoral team or visit us on Sunday to learn more.
