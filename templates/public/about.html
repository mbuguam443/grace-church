Statement of Faith

What We Believe

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God (2 Timothy 3:16).

The Trinity

We believe in one God, eternally existent in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Jesus Christ

We believe in the deity of Christ, His virgin birth, sinless life, miracles, atoning death, and bodily resurrection.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, not by works (Ephesians 2:8-9).

The Church

We believe the Church is the Body of Christ, called to worship, fellowship, discipleship, and mission.

The Second Coming

We believe in the personal, visible return of Jesus Christ and the hope of eternal life for believers.
