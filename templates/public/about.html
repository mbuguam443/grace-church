Our Story

A Legacy of Faith Since 1995

Grace Church Munyaka is a growing branch of Fruitful Brethren Ministry International (FBMI). FBMI was founded in 1995 by Pastor John and Mrs. Mary Mbugua with a small group of 25 believers meeting in a rented hall. What began as a humble gathering has grown into a thriving movement of over 2,000 members, touching countless lives across the nation and beyond.

Our journey has been marked by God's faithfulness, miraculous breakthroughs, and an unwavering commitment to spreading the Gospel. From our first permanent building in 2002 to our current state-of-the-art worship center, every milestone testifies to the goodness of God.

Today, Grace Church Munyaka stands as a beacon of hope in the Munyaka community, a center of excellence in ministry, and a place where lives are transformed by the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our Mission

"To win souls for the Kingdom of God, equip believers for the work of ministry, and transform communities through the demonstration of God's love, power, and truth."

We are committed to reaching the lost, discipling the found, and sending equipped believers to impact every sphere of society for the glory of God.

Our Vision

"To be a Spirit-led, multi-generational church that raises world changers and establishes God's Kingdom in every nation."

We envision a church that transcends cultural and generational barriers, united in purpose and passionate about fulfilling the Great Commission.

Statement of Faith

What We Believe

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God (2 Timothy 3:16).

The Trinity

We believe in one God, eternally existent in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Jesus Christ

We believe in the deity of Christ, His virgin birth, sinless life, miracles, atoning death, and bodily resurrection.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, not by works (Ephesians 2:8-9).

The Church

We believe the Church is the Body of Christ, called to worship, fellowship, discipleship, and mission.

The Second Coming

We believe in the personal, visible return of Jesus Christ and the hope of eternal life for believers.
