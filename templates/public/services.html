Service Times

Join us for worship, prayer, and the ministry of God's Word. We can't wait to welcome you.

When We Meet

Weekly Services

We gather multiple times each week for worship, prayer, and fellowship. Find a service that fits your schedule.

Sunday First Service

8:00 AM

A reverent, traditional worship experience with hymns, prayer, and the ministry of God's Word. Sunday School for all ages begins at 9:30 AM.

  • Holy Communion (1st Sunday)
  • Children's Church Available
  • Nursery Care Provided
Main Service

Sunday Second Service

11:00 AM

Our flagship service featuring contemporary worship, dynamic praise, and powerful preaching. This is our most attended service with full choir and worship team.

  • Full Worship Team & Choir
  • Children's Church Available
  • Live Streamed Online

Wednesday Midweek

7:00 PM

A powerful midweek service focused on Bible teaching, prayer, and spiritual renewal. Perfect for recharging your faith in the middle of the week.

  • In-Depth Bible Teaching
  • Prayer & Intercession
  • Testimonies & Praise
First Time Visiting?

What to Expect

We understand that visiting a new church can be nerve-wracking. Here's what you can expect when you come to Grace Church Munyaka.

1
Warm Welcome

Our greeters will welcome you at the door and help you find your way. You'll feel at home from the moment you arrive.

2
Powerful Worship

Experience heartfelt worship through music that ushers you into God's presence. Sing along or simply soak in the atmosphere.

3
Life-Changing Word

Receive practical, Bible-based preaching that speaks to your everyday life and equips you for your God-given purpose.

4
Fellowship

Connect with friendly people who genuinely care. Enjoy refreshments and conversations after the service.

Special Gatherings

Special Services & Events

All-Night Prayer

First Friday of every month, 10:00 PM - 4:00 AM

Join us for an intense night of prayer, worship, and spiritual warfare. Experience breakthroughs through corporate intercession.

Water Baptism

Quarterly - Next: Last Sunday of the quarter

If you've accepted Christ and want to take the next step of water baptism, we hold baptismal services every quarter.

Marriage Enrichment

Bi-annual weekend retreats

Strengthen your marriage through biblical teaching, couple activities, and quality time together in a beautiful retreat setting.

New Members Class

Every quarter, Sundays 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM

A 4-week course for those who want to become official members of Grace Church Munyaka. Learn our beliefs, vision, and how to get involved.
